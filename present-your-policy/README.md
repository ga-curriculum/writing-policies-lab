**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to effectively present a proposed Acceptable Use Policy to relevant stakeholders, articulating its purpose, key components, and alignment with organizational needs.

In this final exercise, you'll practice presenting your AUP proposal to stakeholders.

## Exercise

Imagine you're presenting your draft AUP to a group of company stakeholders (IT leadership, HR, Legal, employee representatives). Prepare a brief (5 minute) presentation that covers:

- The purpose and objectives of the AUP
- Key components of the policy
- How it addresses stakeholder needs and concerns
- Next steps for review and implementation

Consider creating visual aids (slides, flowcharts) to support your key points.

If time allows, deliver your refined presentation to a larger group or your instructor for additional feedback.
