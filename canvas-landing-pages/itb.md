## About

This module provides an introduction to writing effective technology policies, focusing on crafting clear, enforceable Acceptable Use Policies (AUPs). You'll gain a deep understanding of the key elements, best practices, and practical skills needed to analyze policy requirements, write robust policy documents, and communicate policy proposals to diverse stakeholders.
